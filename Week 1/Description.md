**Gamified App For Student**

**1\. What It Is？**

A mobile application for higher education students that uses game-like features to encourage and motivate personal development. The app's core philosophy is to encourage tangible, real-world actions, with a unique focus on providing a tool to practice and improve face-to-face social skills.

**2\. Who It's For？**

This app is for university and college students who are navigating the challenges of balancing academic life with personal well-being. It is designed to help with:

Consolidating personal tasks and goals into one manageable place.

Maintaining motivation for self-directed study.

Overcoming the difficulty of making meaningful social connections on campus.

**3\. What It's Going to Do？**

The application will provide a simple and engaging user experience built around three main pillars:

**Productivity Tools:**

A Personal To-Do List for users to manually add and track any task (academic, personal, etc.).

A Timetable for class scheduling and reminders.

A "Focus Mode" that rewards students with points for completing study sessions without phone distraction.

**Real-World Activities:**

A Step Counter to encourage daily physical activity.

A unique Bluetooth-based Social Feature to reward confirmed face-to-face interactions with other students, promoting a supportive campus community. This feature will be anonymous and require user consent to protect privacy.

**Gamification Engine:**

Users earn Points and unlock Badges for completing tasks and activities.

A "League-based" Leaderboard System places users in small, competitive groups, ensuring that the ranking is always motivating and provides an achievable goal for everyone.
